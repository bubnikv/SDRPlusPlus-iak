SDR++ IARU regional supplements

Missing amateur-band ranges are supplemented from official IARU regional
band plans. These are advisory operating plans, not authorization to
transmit; national regulations always prevail.
International Amateur Radio Union Region 1: IARU-R1 VHF Handbook, VHF Handbook 10.02
Source: https://www.iaru-r1.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/VHF_Handbook_V10_02.pdf
International Amateur Radio Union Region 2: IARU Region 2 Band Plan, September 2020
Source: https://www.iaru-r2.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/IARU-Region-2-Band-plan.pdf
International Amateur Radio Union Region 3: Band Plans IARU Region 3, R3-004 Rev. 2, November 2024
Source: https://www.iaru-r3.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/R3-004-Band-Plans-IARU-Region-3.pdf
